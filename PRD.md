# 🚀 NEXA - NexovTech Growth AI Agent

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

---

# Vision

NEXA (NexovTech Growth AI Agent) is an AI-powered business growth platform designed to help NexovTech automate lead generation, client acquisition, business intelligence, sales operations, content creation, and strategic decision-making.

The goal is to create an intelligent virtual business partner that continuously works to identify opportunities, generate leads, analyze company performance, and recommend actions that drive growth.

---

# Core Objectives

* Automate lead discovery
* Improve client acquisition
* Reduce manual sales efforts
* Generate business insights
* Automate content marketing
* Increase conversion rates
* Scale NexovTech operations
